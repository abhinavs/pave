Most "starter templates" start you at zero. You still have to choose an auth
library, wire migrations, pick a job queue, and figure out how to deploy. A
week later you have made no product progress.

Pave starts you further along. The day you clone it, these already work and
already work *together*:

- Email and OAuth auth, sessions, password reset
- Async SQLAlchemy with Alembic migrations
- A Soniq job queue with a worked example
- HTMX components for the patterns you actually reach for
- Ruff for lint and format, mypy in strict mode on `app/`
- A Fabric deploy pipeline that runs ruff, mypy, and pytest before any SSH

## No JavaScript build

The interactive bits are HTMX and a little Alpine. There is no bundler, no
`node_modules`, no hydration step. The webhook demo on the home page is the
reference: live search over a table, server-rendered, swapped in place.
