## Who this covers

This policy describes how this site handles information when you use it. If you
have deployed Pave for your own product, "we" means you, the operator of this
site, and you are responsible for keeping this page accurate.

## What we collect

Out of the box, Pave stores only what it needs to run:

- **Account information.** When you sign up, we store your name, email
  address, and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google or
  GitHub instead, we store the provider name and the identifier that provider
  gives us, plus your name and email from your profile.
- **Session records.** When you log in, we create a session so you stay signed
  in. The cookie in your browser is opaque; only a one-way hash of it is
  stored on the server, alongside the device and approximate time of sign-in
  so you can review and revoke your own active sessions.
- **Content you submit.** Anything you send to the application, such as the
  payloads delivered to the demo webhook endpoint, is stored so the app can
  process and display it.
- **Operational logs.** Standard server logs may record request metadata such
  as IP address, user agent, and timestamps. These help with debugging,
  security, and abuse prevention.

## What we do not collect

Pave ships with no analytics, no third-party trackers, and no advertising
networks. We do not sell your data, and there is nothing here that profiles
you across other sites. If you add any of these, update this page to say so
plainly.

## How we use it

We use the information above to operate the service: to authenticate you, keep
you signed in, deliver the features you ask for, and keep the system secure and
working. We do not use it for anything you have not asked for.

## Third parties

By default, the only third parties involved are the ones you choose to turn on:

- **OAuth providers** (Google, GitHub) if you sign in with them.
- **An email provider** for confirmation and password-reset messages, if you
  have configured one.

Each of these only receives what it needs to do its job. List any others you
add here.

## Cookies

Pave uses a single session cookie to keep you signed in. It is marked
`HttpOnly` and `SameSite=Lax`, and `Secure` in production. There are no
advertising or tracking cookies.

## Data retention

Account and content records are kept until you delete them or close your
account. Operational logs are kept only as long as they are useful for
debugging and security. Set a concrete retention window here that matches how
you actually run the service.
