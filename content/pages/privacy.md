This is placeholder copy that ships with the Pave template. Replace it before
you launch.

Out of the box Pave stores only what it needs to run: your account record
(name, email, an optional OAuth provider id) and any webhook payloads you
send to the demo endpoint. Sessions are signed cookies, not server-side
records.

Pave does not include analytics, third-party trackers, or ad networks. If you
add them, update this page to say so honestly.
